Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Learn how KTECH collects and handles your enquiry and quote information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to KTECH Home Appliances. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal contact information you provide while exploring our product catalogue and submitting commercial enquiries.

Important Notice: This website is an official product catalogue and lead-generation portal. We do not conduct online transactions, process credit cards, or operate customer checkout accounts on this platform.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a contact enquiry or request a quotation, we collect:

  • Your Full Name and Company / Organization name
  • Mobile and WhatsApp contact phone number
  • Email address and delivery city/state
  • Product models of interest, estimated quantities, and customization requirements

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your contact details solely for commercial communication regarding your request:

  • Preparing formal pricing quotations and product catalogues
  • Connecting via phone, email, or WhatsApp to confirm product availability and logistics
  • Coordinating delivery schedules and factory dispatch details
  • We never sell, lease, or monetize your contact information to third parties.

4. Data Security & Storage

All submitted enquiries are stored within our secured database systems. Access is restricted strictly to authorized KTECH commercial and sales desk personnel.
